## Observer Summary Notes

**Biggest pain points observed:**

- Product catalog feels thin — tester immediately noticed the limited number of items and commented on it
- Portfolio page intent was unclear at first glance — tester needed time to figure out what the site was for
- GitHub/SyncStream links on portfolio were confusing as a starting point — not immediately readable as portfolio projects

**Bugs to fix (priority order):**

- No bugs found during testing session 🎉
- Consider adding more seeded products to make the store feel fuller
- Portfolio CTA for sample websites may need to be more prominent

**Positive feedback to keep:**

- Checkout flow was fast and intuitive — tester completed a purchase with zero guidance
- Business sites looked professional enough that tester asked "wait, is this a real website?"
- UI navigation / menu praised as easy to use

**Ideas for future features:**

- Support page or suggestions/feedback form for MoCashRoCo
- Brighter, more colorful visual theme ("poppy, not dark + mysterious")
- More products / fuller catalog for a more realistic shopping experience
